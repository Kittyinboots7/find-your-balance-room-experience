Playback processing: short equal-power crossfades at loop boundaries, DC removal, level matching with peak headroom, gentle low/high-frequency shaping, soft compression, and gradual fade-in/out. The fireplace receives warmer filtering to soften sharp high-frequency crackles. The card-reading level remains softer than the room level without becoming inaudible.

These are illustrative ambient recordings, not recordings made at the hotel. Sound begins only after entering a room, and the visitor can mute it at any time.
